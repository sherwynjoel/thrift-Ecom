# Shipping & Delivery Policy

## Where we ship

We deliver everywhere in India. We do not ship outside India yet.

## Processing time

Every tee, ready-made or custom-printed, is printed or packed and handed to the courier within **1 to 2 working days** of payment.

## Delivery time (after dispatch)

- **Metro cities:** 2 to 4 days
- **Rest of India:** 4 to 7 days

Remote areas can take a little longer. Delivery times are estimates from our courier partners, not guarantees.

## Delivery charges

- **Free delivery** on orders over ₹999.
- Below that, a flat **₹79** delivery fee is added at checkout.

All orders are prepaid. We do not offer cash on delivery.

## Tracking

As soon as the courier picks up your parcel, we email you a tracking link. You can also see the order status any time under **Account → Orders**.

## Problems with delivery

If your parcel is delayed, lost or arrives damaged, email tboxcbe@gmail.com with your order number. We will chase the courier and, if the parcel is lost, send a replacement or a full refund.
