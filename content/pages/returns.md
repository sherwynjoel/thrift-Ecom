# Refund & Cancellation Policy

## Cancelling an order

- You can cancel any order **before it ships**. Email tboxcbe@gmail.com with your order number. We cancel it and refund the **full amount**.
- Once an order has shipped it can no longer be cancelled. You can return it after delivery as described below.
- Custom-printed orders can be cancelled only before printing starts, usually within a few hours of ordering.
- We may cancel an order ourselves if an item goes out of stock or a custom design breaks our content rules. You get a full refund.

## Returns and exchanges

- You have **7 days from delivery** to request a return or a size exchange on ready-made tees.
- Items must be unworn, unwashed, with tags on and in the original packaging.
- **Custom-printed tees** are made only for you and cannot be returned or exchanged, unless they arrive damaged, defective or wrongly printed.
- To start a return or exchange, email tboxcbe@gmail.com with your order number and the reason. We will arrange a pickup.

## Damaged or wrong items

If your order arrives damaged, defective or not what you ordered, email us within **48 hours of delivery** with your order number and photos. We will replace it or refund it in full, at no cost to you.

## Refunds

- Refunds go back to the **original payment method** (UPI, card, net banking or wallet) through Razorpay.
- We start the refund within **2 working days** of cancelling your order, or of receiving and checking the returned item.
- The money reaches your account within **5 to 7 working days** after that, depending on your bank.
- Delivery charges are refunded when the return is because of our mistake (damaged, defective or wrong item).

## Contact

Email tboxcbe@gmail.com with any question about cancellations, returns or refunds.
