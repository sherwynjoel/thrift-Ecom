# Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal information tebox.in collects, why, and what control you have over it.

## What we collect

- **Account details:** your name, email address and password (stored only as a secure hash). If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email from Google.
- **Order details:** delivery address, phone number and the items you buy.
- **Designs you upload:** artwork and text you add in the design studio.
- **Payment details:** handled entirely by our payment partner, Razorpay. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details.
- **Usage data:** basic information about how the site is used, to keep it working and improve it.

## How we use it

- To process, print, ship and support your orders.
- To send order emails: confirmation, shipping updates and refunds.
- To keep your bag, wishlist and login working.
- To send offers or newsletters, only if you sign up for them. You can unsubscribe at any time.

## Who we share it with

We share only what each partner needs to do its job:

- **Razorpay**, to take payments and issue refunds.
- **Courier partners**, your name, address and phone number, to deliver your order.
- **Email and hosting providers**, to run the site and send emails.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

## Cookies

We use cookies to keep you logged in, remember your bag and protect the site. Turning cookies off in your browser may stop the bag and login from working.

## How long we keep it

We keep order records as long as tax and accounting law requires. Other account data is kept while your account is active.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data. Email tboxcbe@gmail.com from the address on your account and we will reply within 7 working days.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page.
